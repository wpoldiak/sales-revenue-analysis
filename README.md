**Retail Sales & Financial Analysis
Overview**

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify trends, evaluate profitability, and measure financial performance. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, the goal is to turn raw data into actionable business insights and recommendations.

**Business Objectives**

- Analyze revenue, profit, and profit margins.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products and regions.
- Evaluate monthly sales trends and growth.
- Compare actual financial performance against budget targets.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.

**Tools & Technologies**

- Python: Data cleaning, analysis, and visualization
- pandas: Data manipulation and exploration
- SQL: Data querying and business analysis
- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and reporting
- Excel: Financial analysis and validation
- GitHub: Version control and project documentation

**Project Structure**

- data/ — Raw and processed datasets
- notebooks/ — Python notebooks for cleaning and analysis
- sql/ — SQL queries used to answer business questions
- powerbi/ — Dashboard files and screenshots
- reports/ — Findings and business recommendations

**Key Business Questions**

1. Which products and regions generate the most revenue and profit?
2. How do revenue and profit margins change over time?
3. Which categories or regions have the weakest profitability?
4. How does actual performance compare with budget targets?
5. What actions could improve financial performance?

**Methodology**

1. Inspect and clean the raw sales dataset.
2. Validate data quality and financial calculations.
3. Analyze sales and profitability using SQL.
4. Calculate financial metrics and budget variances.
5. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
6. Summarize findings and develop business recommendations.
